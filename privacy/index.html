Privacy Policy

Wizard Computer Trading · Ras Al Khaimah, UAE · last updated 24 October 2026

This page explains what information we keep when you visit our shop, message us, or buy from us, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we keep very little.

Who we are

Wizard Computer Trading, Shop 2, Ground Floor, Al Mera Building, Khuzam Road, Al Qurm, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE (opposite RAK Mall). Phone 07 228 0220 · WhatsApp 054 998 2888 · sales@wizardtech.ae.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, when you book a repair, ask for a quote or buy something.
  • Device details: model, fault description and the repair notes we write, so we can fix it and stand behind our warranty.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, including photos and voice notes, so our team can reply.
  • Invoices and payments, as UAE tax law requires. We never see or keep full card numbers; card payments go through the bank's terminal.

We do not look at the personal files on a device you leave with us, except when the repair needs it (for example, checking data was copied correctly) and only with your permission.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages, prepare quotes and tell you when your device is ready.
  • To keep invoices and warranty records.
  • Some first replies on WhatsApp and Instagram (opening hours, location, repair status) are sent automatically, and a staff member takes over the rest.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising lists.

Who else handles it

Only the services we use to run the shop: our accounting system (Zoho Books), WhatsApp, and Instagram/Facebook (Meta) for messages. Our message inbox runs on the shop's own server. We share information with anyone else only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Invoices and tax records: as long as UAE law requires (at least 5 years). Chat messages and repair notes: as long as they are useful for your repair or warranty, then we delete them.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp 054 998 2888 or email sales@wizardtech.ae with your name and phone number. We will reply within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (such as tax invoices) stay until that period ends.

Instagram and Facebook: to remove our access, open Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites (or Facebook → Settings → Business integrations) and remove "Wizard Posting". Then send us a message asking us to delete your conversation history, and we will.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
